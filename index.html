Principal Research Scientist · Common Crawl Foundation

Pedro Ortiz Suarez

I work on large corpora for training language models, with a particular focus on under-resourced and historical languages. My research spans corpus linguistics, language modeling, named entity recognition, dependency parsing, and machine translation.
